# Structured prompt experiment — plan written before inference

Goal: an illustrative continuous surface + subsurface scene, never field footage, diagnosis or simulation. Test prompt composition, not the previously rejected 2D single-tile guidance. Fixed tripod at an actual excavated sidewalk edge, natural daylight, full scale. Surface pavers and exposed earth must belong to the same spatial structure, visible together, no invisible-cause claims. In the event, loose surface support settles and the paver field dips as a group; soil and bedding are visible only where an existing inspection trench exposes them. No pipe, water, exposed void or cause is asserted. Timing ideal: first quarter stable; middle half slow event; last quarter rests. Camera, concrete identity, trench, boundary and lighting must stay fixed. Time compression is illustrative.

Same Wan2.2 TI2V 5B model, seed 2026102912, 640x384, 49 frames, 20 steps, CFG5, uni_pc/simple, shift8. Three T2V hypotheses: A wide three-quarter open trench with paving above (complete-scene experiment), B horizontal section without unnatural plinth (complete-scene experiment), C surface-only documentary control (diagnostic; never complete). A/B use different scene staging, not a controlled single-variable intervention; C tests surface recognizability only. Same common negative. Expected failures: plinth, floating pavers or giant hole; new block/hatch or unstable identity; tiny/no motion; misleading cross-section; camera drift, flicker, malformed aggregate. A single seed cannot establish general quality. Compare actual decoded every-frame grids and full resolution samples. Gate: recognizable contiguous surface and support, coherent visible controlled downward event, rigid individual shapes, fixed background, believable scale and materials, no fabricated cause. Reject if missing any; no UI integration until real-time human and technical review. Sampling not deterministic across environments.
