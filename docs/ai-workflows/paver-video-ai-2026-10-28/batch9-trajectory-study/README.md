# Batch 9: native Wan-Move trajectory study — 2026-10-29

## Outcome

Two actual local model inferences, **not guide renders**. Both fail the desired convincing rigid-block settlement and fail the complete explanatory-sequence gate. They are synthetic surface-only illustrations, not footage, simulation, construction details or evidence of a pipe, layers, subsurface cause or diagnosis. No preview, route or production changes.

- `move-12.mp4`: nominal 12-pixel downward target. Viewed all 49 frames together in `grid-12.jpg` and original-size frames 0, 24, 48: camera/outer pattern appears broadly stable, but the main visible change is an unnatural dark slit growing beneath a block in the upper middle and slight joint/texture changes. No clearly believable independent rigid block lowering; endpoint is not unchanged or clamped. Fail.
- `move-24.mp4`: nominal 24-pixel target, otherwise same seed and graph. Same frame inspection: substantially more obvious black slit opening across the middle, neighboring joints and apparent block shapes move/change. Appears like separation/opening, not clear physical lowering of rigid blocks. Outer frame broadly stable; fail.

Neither should be integrated. Grid frames are downsized; sampled stills are 640×400. This was **not human real-time playback**; subtle flicker, deformation and motion quality remain unverified. End frames establish no hold after the event. The input view is almost overhead, so screen-space downward paths do not guarantee physical vertical motion. An independent overlay of the input coordinates shows all nine moving points **start within the same visible block**; in the 24-pixel condition some endpoints extend across the original lower joint. Crossing an original image boundary is not, by itself, proof of an error in a moving-object guide, but the resulting black gap remains visually unconvincing. These are limitations of this experiment, not evidence against all trajectory methods. 12 versus 24 compares control displacement, not random seeds.

## Method and exact execution

Native ComfyUI `WanMoveTracksFromCoords` and `WanMoveTrackToVideo` propagate start-image features along manually specified tracks. Nine points in/around the center move downward quadratically by 12 or 24 image pixels over 49 frames; nine surrounding anchor points remain fixed. Full coordinates and prompts are in `case-12.json` and `case-24.json`; tracks are model conditioning, **not physical constraints or generated video**. 640×400, 49 frames, seed 2026102940, 20 steps, CFG 5, uni_pc/simple, shift 8, strength 1; H.264 CRF20 yuv420p, 16 fps. `case-*-history.json` contains actual server execution histories. `run.py` captures the exact submitted graphs and coordinates and safety code; `template.json` is official ComfyUI Wan-Move template downloaded from https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Comfy-Org/workflow_templates/main/templates/video_wanmove_480p.json (mutable source; retained snapshot). Unlike the template, no turbo LoRA was used. `start.png` is the prior generated RealVisXL reference 4, not either rejected Qwen edit; input provenance and commercial/attribution conditions for RealVisXL and SDXL VAE are in `docs/paver-hybrid-provenance-2026-10-28.md` and main experiment provenance. Source is synthetic and not ground truth.

Server: ComfyUI `cdf272f3514353aab04dc2d9fceef57a260dc156`, installed native nodes, NVIDIA GB10. Two sequential inferences: 274.1 s and 262.1 s; sampled minimum system MemAvailable 80071 and 80465 MiB respectively, not guaranteed peak GPU consumption.
